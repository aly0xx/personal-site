## Summary
- A depressing novel about the life of the english literature professor William Stoner who initially enters university to study agriculture but is enamored by his love for literature so he switches major. He falls in love with a sickly woman who becomes repulsed by him and eventually fights him for custody of their daughter. 

- He engages in an affair with a collegue but his boss who's also his rival finds out and forces him to end it, meanwhile Stones gets grueling and uninteresting schedules and becomes more unhappy year by year.

## Notes

- A very evocative writing style that's very realistic. It made me realise that not everyone ends up living a happy life even though they didn't do anything wrong and that you have to take care of family even when its deeply unsettling.
