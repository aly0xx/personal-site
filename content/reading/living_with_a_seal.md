## Summary
- Jesse becomes bored and overwhelmed by his routine and wants to exercise more and be in better shape (even though he runs everyday and has run multiple 100-mile races) so he asks David Goggins to come live him for a month and chronicles their adventures together.

## Notes

- I really enjoyed reading this book to be honest and really liked Jesse's writing style as I enjoyed his podcase episode with Joe Rogan. It shows that the boundaries and limits you set on yourself are much lower than you're truly capable of.
