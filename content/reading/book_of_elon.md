## Summary
- Eric went through all of Elon Musk's thoughts, ideas and advice from countless interviews, podcasts, articles etc and formatted them into a digestible book. From his first principles and thinking in terms of limits approaches, to how to build and inspire teams with a sense of urgency to build important stuff.


## Notes
- This is an incredible book, even though I've watched many of Elon Musk's interviews, podcasts and read several books on him his ideas have enver been structured this organised before, whenever you get stuck on something you can flick through the contents page and find what you need with pieces of timeless advice that can help you build billion dollar businesses.
